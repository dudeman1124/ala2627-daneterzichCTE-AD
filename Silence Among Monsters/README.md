# Silence Among Monsters

A first-person bedroom prototype set in a room that is 20 feet wide, 25 feet long, and 8 feet tall.

## Play

Open `index.html` from the project directory, or serve the repository and visit `/Silence%20Among%20Monsters/`. The 3D scene uses Three.js from jsDelivr, so an internet connection is needed to load it.

- **W A S D** — move
- **Mouse** — look around
- **E** — use a wall switch while aiming at it
- **H** — hide under the bed when nearby; press again to come out
- **Esc** — pause or return the mouse cursor

The two wall switches turn the bedroom lights and ceiling fan on and off independently.
